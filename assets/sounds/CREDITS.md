# Sound credits

The animal calls in `animals/` are short excerpts of recordings from Wikimedia
Commons, trimmed and levelled (ffmpeg loudnorm) for Who Said That? and the
Animal Piano's free play. Each keeps its original licence; the CC BY-SA ones are
shared under the same licence.
