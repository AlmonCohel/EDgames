Four looks for the same site

Chunky was picked in October 2026 and is the live site now. The other three stay here as options for a future refresh. All the games, the subject row, the shelves, the age and search filters, and the game pages. Only the look changes. Each one is the real site with one extra stylesheet on top, so everything works: filter, search, open any game and play it.

Live now: Chunky

The live site עכשיו

Chunky, as built for real, with the illustrated covers.

The app layout stays exactly as it is in all four looks: the subject row, the continue card, the shelves, the bottom bar and the age and search filters.

Look 1

Chunky לחיץ

Every tile and button is a fat toy button that squashes when pressed.

Thick darker edges under everything, rounder letters (Fredoka), brighter subject colours, tiles that pop in one after another, a big round play button on the continue card, and a floating bottom tray.

Feels like
Duolingo-style; tactile and very tappable
Look 2

Sticker pop מדבקות

Comic outlines, and each game is a die-cut sticker.

Each shelf sits on its own bright band with a scalloped top. Tiles have a thick white sticker border and lean slightly left or right. Headings look like stuck-on labels, the top bar is yellow and the bottom bar is a black comic panel.

Feels like
The loudest and most playful; a sticker album
Look 3

Jelly ג׳לי

Soft pastel sky, and every game is a glossy candy.

Tiles are shiny jelly sweets with a shine across the top and a soft coloured glow, and they wobble like jelly as they appear. The bars are frosted glass, and the bottom bar is a floating glass capsule.

Feels like
Soft, sweet and calm; the gentlest
Look 4

Big pictures תמונות גדולות

Made for a child who can’t read yet: bigger pictures, fewer words in the way.

Subjects become big round story bubbles, like on Instagram. The continue card becomes a large hero with a המשך button. Tiles are bigger with the name on the picture, and each subject shelf has its own tile shape (round, square, tall). Search is a big round button.

Feels like
A kids’ app store; the most modern
What these are. The three looks that were not picked sit on a frozen copy of the stylesheet from before Chunky (mockups/v3/base.css), so they still look the way they did. Picking one later means its skin.css goes into assets/styles.css.
